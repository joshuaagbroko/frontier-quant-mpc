Framework

After each MPC meeting, the CBN publishes a personal statement from every committee member. I read all statements and assign a hawk score of 1–10 to each member using a documented rubric: language markers such as describing inflation as "entrenched" or "demand-driven" score positively; language emphasising growth risk or incomplete transmission scores negatively. The composite is the mean hawk score across all scored members.

A second signal — the FMDQ 3-month forward premium — tracks the market's implied naira depreciation expectation. A widening spread historically precedes MPC caution about further easing. A third signal, the asymmetric corridor width, captures the CBN's liquidity stance independently of the headline MPR: a narrow upper band signals accommodation before the committee acknowledges it in rate decisions.

The composite is a communication tracker, not a vote predictor. When the composite diverges from market expectations, it identifies a gap worth explaining — whether or not that gap resolves in the announced decision. Full methodology and historical data available on Substack.
